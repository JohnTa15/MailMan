Mailman privacy policy

Mailman is a self-hosted email and calendar app. This server is run privately by its owner for the people they invite. There is no company behind it, no advertising and no analytics.

What Mailman stores

  • Your Mailman account: name, email address and a hashed password.
  • For each mailbox you connect: its address, server settings, and the sign-in tokens or app password needed to reach it, stored encrypted.
  • Copies of your email messages, so you can read and search them. Calendar events are read when you open the calendar and are not stored.

Google and Microsoft data

When you connect a Google or Microsoft account, Mailman asks for access to your mail (to read, organise and send it on your behalf) and read-only access to your calendars (to show your events). This data is used only to show it to you in Mailman. It is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Mailman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI assistants

Mailman only connects an AI assistant to your mail if you create an AI key yourself in Settings. You choose what it may do, it can never send email, and you can revoke the key at any time.

Removing your data

Removing a mailbox in Settings deletes its messages and tokens from this server. You can also revoke Mailman's access at any time from your Google account or Microsoft account. To delete your Mailman account, ask the person who runs this server.

Mailman is open source.
